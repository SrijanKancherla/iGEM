7. SCORE INTERPRETATION GUIDE
================================================================================

FINAL SCORE (Combined Ranking):
  ≥ 0.75  : Excellent candidate - high confidence
  0.6-0.75: Good candidate - recommended for synthesis
  0.5-0.6 : Acceptable - worth validating
  < 0.5   : Risky - low confidence, many trade-offs

DDG (Thermostability, kcal/mol):
  < -5    : Highly stabilizing (very good)
  -3 to -5: Good improvement
  -1 to -3: Modest improvement
  > 0     : Destabilizing (bad)

SOLUBILITY SCORE (E. coli Expression, 0-10):
  ≥ 8     : Safe for E. coli expression
  5-8     : Monitor; may aggregate under high expression
  < 5     : High aggregation risk; avoid

EPITOPE PENALTIES (Immune Escape):
  0       : Safe - outside epitope region
  1-10    : Minor risk - near epitope
  > 10    : High risk - inside epitope region
